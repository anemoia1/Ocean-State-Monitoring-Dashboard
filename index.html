Sea Level & Salinity

Sea Level Rise

Global mean sea level has risen by +115 mm since 1990, with the rate accelerating from 1.4 mm/yr in 1993 to over 3.7 mm/yr in 2024. Projections suggest 0.3–1.0 m rise by 2100 under current emissions.

Chemical Oceanography

Acidification & Oxygen

As CO₂ dissolves into seawater, oceans acidify — threatening calcifying organisms and entire food webs. Deoxygenation compounds the crisis.

⚗️ Acidity

Ocean Acidification (pH)

Surface ocean pH has declined from a pre-industrial 8.18 to the current 8.04 — a 30% increase in hydrogen ion concentration. Shell-forming organisms face dissolution stress.
